# Gaming for Investors

My app helps Investors decide which studies to invest in by analyzing commercial success, highest content output, and potential pricing power.

> A course project for **MIST 4600: Computer Programming in Business** at the University of Georgia's Terry College of Business (Fall 2026).

## Problem
Our users are investors who want to look past financials, and analyze the productivity and success of various game devs. This information could be used to help make investment decisions. 

## What it does
My app answers three questions:
1. **Sub-question 1:** Which developers produce the most downloaded and played games?
2. **Sub-question 2:** Which developers are publishing high value content on a regular basis?
3. **Sub-question 3:** What is the average price a user could expect to pay after purchasing the game and buying cosmetics (using llm api).

## Data
The [Steam Games Dataset](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/artermiloff/steam-games-dataset) on Kaggle, collected in March 2025. The dataset is shared under the MIT License by its creator, artermiloff. This repo uses `steam_games.csv`, a smaller file made for the course: the 1,000 games with the most reviews.
